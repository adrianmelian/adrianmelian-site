**Arizona Sunshine: Contagion Z (2019) | Nomadic VR**

This was a **location-based VR experience**, where users use advanced, tetherless headsets, haptic gear, and motion tracking to explore virtual worlds.

As a **Technical Artist**, I contributed across **modeling, rigging, animation, gameplay scripting, 3D scanning & 3D printing**, supporting rapid iteration and keeping assets production-ready for LBE VR performance constraints.

On the technical side, I also worked on **gameplay scripting** that interfaced with physical components like **servos, gyros, and actuators**, helping synchronize real-world motion and effects with what the player was seeing and feeling in VR.
