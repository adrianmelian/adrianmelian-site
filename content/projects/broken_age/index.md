**Broken Age | Double Fine Productions | Technical Artist | 2016**

On Broken Age, I created the **Flip Book Animation System** used for *all* character animation in the game.

This was a custom rigging + animation setup built around a hybrid approach: **hand-drawn 2D parts** were stitched onto **flat 3D meshes**, allowing the characters to deform like a rig while still keeping that illustrated, storybook look.
