**Meta Horizon Worlds | Meta | Technical Artist | 2021**

On Meta Horizon Worlds, I worked as a **Technical Artist** focused on building tools and workflows that helped teams ship faster, especially under the unique constraints of **VR performance** and a rapidly changing environment.

A big part of my work was creating **artist-facing tools** that made production smoother and much less painful. One highlight was a robust **Animation Exporter** that integrated with **ShotGrid** to keep communication and approvals flowing, automatically updating animation statuses and sending directors playblasts for review. The best compliment: when I returned to Meta years later, the tool was **still in active use**.

I also built **performance-optimized VR workflows** and tooling to capture and report performance statistics, helping teams make informed trade-offs between fidelity and frame-time.

Finally, I developed a **Figma-to-ReactVR plugin** to help UI artists iterate quickly and consistently. Turning design intent into usable UI implementations with less friction between art and engineering.

**Key Contributions**
- Built a robust **Animation Exporter** with **ShotGrid integration** (status updates + automated playblast delivery).
- Created tools and workflows for **VR performance optimization**, including capture/reporting of performance statistics.
- Developed a **Figma → ReactVR plugin** to accelerate UI iteration and reduce handoff friction.
