Krazy Kaiju! is a personal VR game project I’ve been building in my free time using **Unreal Engine**. The vibe is playful, weird, and slightly unhinged (the best kind): a lonely farmer defending his farm from an alien invasion.

This is a true from-scratch project, I built **everything**: concepting, modeling, texturing, rigging, animation, gameplay engineering, and even a full mini-pipeline to keep the project organized and scalable as it grows.

And I made the music too. 🙂
