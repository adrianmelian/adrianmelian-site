Call of Duty - MW3 | Sledgehammer Games | Lead Technical Artist | 2023

On Modern Warfare III, I led the Character Tech Art at Sledgehammer Games. Supporting a high-volume AAA character pipeline where quality, speed, and performance all have to land at the same time.

In addition to day-to-day production leadership, I also pushed forward **ML research for auto-skinning** as an exploration into faster, more consistent character deformation workflows.

**Key Contributions**
- Led the Character Tech Art team; trained and managed internal and outsource artists.
- Developed and maintained character, animation, and rigging workflows to support large-scale production.
- Created proprietary tools for animation, rigging, game preview, and cloth simulation to improve iteration speed and reliability.
- Skinned high-fidelity characters, including celebrity likenesses (yes, including **Snoop Dogg**).
- Balanced and optimized character assets for in-engine performance while protecting deformation quality.
