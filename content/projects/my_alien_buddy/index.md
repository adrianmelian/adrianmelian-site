**My Alien Buddy (DLC for Playroom PS4) | Double Fine Productions | Technical Artist | 2013**

This project was **challenging in the best way**, we were working on early PS4 devkits, and **ASOBI Engine** documentation was barely in existence, and in Japanese.

I handled **modeling, rigging, and animation** for the main **Alien** character and the **UFO**, and I also scripted the **intro sequence in Lua**, wiring up the beats so everything hit the right timing and felt polished.

It was a crash course in making things work when the platform is brand new and the rulebook is still being written. Great experience.
