My Alien Buddy (DLC for Playroom PS4) | Double Fine Productions | Technical Artist | 2013

This project was **challenging in the most classic way**: we were working on early PS4 devkits, and engine documentation was… let’s call it *aspirational*.

I handled **modeling, rigging, and animation** for the main **Alien** character and the **UFO**, and I also scripted the **intro sequence in Lua**, wiring up the beats so everything hit the right timing and felt polished.

It was a crash course in making things work when the platform is brand new and the rulebook is still being written. Tough project, great experience.
