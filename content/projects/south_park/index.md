**South Park - Fractured But Whole | Ubisoft | Senior Technical Artist | 2017**

On South Park: The Fractured But Whole, I worked as a **Senior Technical Artist**, supporting character animation systems and tackling some especially fun rigging challenges.

I contributed to **flipbook animation tooling** and rigged the **final boss**, which included multiple **transformation states**.

I also had the once-in-a-lifetime opportunity to spend time in LA working out of the **Casa Bonita (South Park) studio**.
