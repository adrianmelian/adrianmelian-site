Hi, I'm Adrian Melian. I design the systems other artists ship through. Fourteen years in AAA character rigging, skinning and tools across six proprietary engines. I joined Sledgehammer Games as a Senior Technical Artist on Vanguard and was promoted to Lead. I led Character Tech Art, then Weapons Tech Art, across two Call of Duty titles. Then Camouflaj as an Expert Technical Artist. Before Sledgehammer: Meta, Double Fine, Ubisoft, and NCSoft.

In 2026 I founded FabricatorStudio and shipped its Maya pipeline toolset. I launched Armature as a standalone rigging and skinning app in August 2026.

Away from the screen I paint, draw from life, and build things. See the gallery →

Artist+AI Rigging. You drive.

A standalone rigging and skinning app, no Maya licence needed. A developer takes a mesh to a working rig in minutes; a character artist pushes that same rig to production level. Free for personal work and small studios.
